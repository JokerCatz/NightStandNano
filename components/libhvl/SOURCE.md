- Patch `patches/0001-*.patch` (applied in `hvl/`): `int8` is `signed char` (plain char is unsigned on RISC-V); the 400 KB wave table is allocated while a tune is loaded (`hvl_TablesAcquire/Release`), not a static; `period_tab` padded to 256 entries and `Offsets` to 8 (every uint8 index in range), the header declarations are extern (the header defined its tables in every includer), the filter position and wave length clamped, a position jump past the end ends the song, the tune block zero-filled (undefined tracks are empty), a per-voice mute mask in the mixer.
- File-structure checks for hostile files are in `components/player_core/src/pc_backend_hvl.c` (`hvl_validate`), not in the vendored code.
- The `.bss` of the library (the two pan tables, 2 KB) goes to PSRAM through `linker.lf`.
- Patch `patches/0002-waveforms-subsong-starts-tables-lock.patch` (FIX6, 2026-11-18, wave-6 review): HVL sub-song starts are clamped like the AHX loader does; plist waveforms 5..7 become 0 in both loaders and the square slot of `ht_WaveformTab` is never NULL; `PosJumpNote >= TrackLength`; the wave-table count and build in `hvl_tables.c` sit under a spin lock (three tasks open and close tunes).
